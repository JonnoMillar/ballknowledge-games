# Naming checklist

Tests for any name: the site, a game, a rank, a content series. Written
2026-11-14 while choosing the hub name. A name doesn't need to pass every test,
but a fail on the first block rules it out.

## 1. Must pass

- **Say it once, spell it once.** Hear it in a voice note and type it right:
  no apostrophes, no spaces that matter, no spelling variants.
- **Works in the daily sentence.** "Done today's ___?" and "I got 87 on ___"
  should sound natural (Wordle set this pattern).
- **Answers "game name?" in one word.** The comment reply is the name, and
  typing it into Google or TikTok search finds us.
- **No bad reading.** Slang meanings, other languages, what it sounds like
  out loud ("tapped" also means mad in UK slang).
- **Legally clear.** No club, league, FIFA, EA FC, Football Manager,
  Transfermarkt or other mark; check UKIPO and EUIPO, classes 9 and 41.

## 2. Brand and recognition

- **Short enough for a watermark.** Readable in a corner of a phone video in
  half a second; 8 letters or fewer is best.
- **Has a mark.** One letter or one object from the world that works as a
  16 px favicon and an app icon.
- **Handles match.** The same handle is free on TikTok, Instagram, X and
  YouTube, and the domain is the name (an exact .com is best, .gg is fine).
- **Owned in search.** A common word gets buried (other apps, other meanings).
  Fix with a fixed modifier everywhere (`scouted.gg`, `@scoutedfc`) or pick a
  rarer word.
- **Sounds like a game,** not a tool, a news site or an agency.

## 3. Fits the world

- **Belongs in the fiction.** The name, the world (scout's office), the run
  ("Today's assignment"), ranks and game names share one vocabulary.
- **Gives the player a role.** Ideally the name says who you are or what you
  do (you are the scout; you get scouted).
- **Outlives the art.** It still works if the room changes; it isn't tied to
  one game.
- **Covers every game,** including the multiplayer pub games.

## 4. Findable (search and AI)

- The name doesn't need "football" in it. Search engines and AI assistants
  find us through the **descriptor**, page titles, and mentions elsewhere
  (Reddit threads, "best daily football games" lists, creators), not the
  name.
- So every name ships with a fixed descriptor: "___ : daily football games".
- AI assistants recommend what is written about. The lever is mentions,
  not keywords in the name.

## 5. Social, faceless content

- The name is the watermark, the end card and the handle; all three are the
  same word.
- Content series names point back to a game ("Spot the fake transfer" leads to
  Spoofed), so each game name should also pass block 1.
- Visible enough that people ask "game name?", easy enough that the answer
  sticks.
- Works as a hashtag.

## 6. Audience

- Real currency for 16 to 26s, but not slang that dates in a year.
- Not cringe said out loud in a pub or a group chat.
- UK-first or global? A UK-only phrase limits the US and wider audience.

## Use for other decisions

Game names, rank names and content series names take blocks 1, 3 and 5. Rank
names also follow the short-titles rule (1 to 2 words, no jokes).
