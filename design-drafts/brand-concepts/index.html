10Face covers

From R0040, R0041, R0046 · stand-in photos

Six ways to cover a face. The stand-ins here are real players; the real use is Higgsfield action shots of players who don't exist (sprinting, sliding, celebrating), for banners and backgrounds where no real player is named. A covered face reads as a design choice, not a dodge, and it hides AI faces' weak spot.

Higgsfield: full-body action shots of made-up players, about 2 credits each. I'd use kits in the game's colours rather than real club kits: real kits bring the club's crest and sponsor (trademarks), and a fake player in a real Arsenal shirt still reads as "some Arsenal player". Real kits only if you want that risk.

11Photo icons

Replaces the clay miniatures

Icons as real objects: a photographed whistle, armband, corner flag, cut out and run through whichever effect the world uses (halftone for Pop, sepia for Programme, chalk outline for Tactics). Real things from a football ground, so they make sense on sight, and none of them is computer-drawn. Boxes are placeholders: nothing is generated.

Higgsfield: one studio product shot per object on plain grey, no brand marks. About 2 credits each, 8 objects, so about 16 credits. Cut out with the photo pipeline's rembg.
