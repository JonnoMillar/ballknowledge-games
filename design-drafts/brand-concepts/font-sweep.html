Font sweep: the long list

How these were found: Google Fonts' own metadata (1,961 families) filtered to sans and display faces with an 800+ weight, ranked by Google's popularity order; the AI reflex fonts named by design-slop lists (Inter, DM Sans, Space Grotesk, Outfit, Plus Jakarta, Syne, Manrope, Figtree and so on) and anything in the top ~150 removed; brand faces (TikTok, Wix, Reddit, GitHub, Zalando), rounded and sci-fi faces dropped. Each face below sets the wordmark, a tape label, the clipboard header and a task row at its heaviest weight, on the hub art. Rank is Google's popularity position (1 = most used).
