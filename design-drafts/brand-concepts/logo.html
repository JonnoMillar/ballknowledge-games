How complexity is kept down
  • One stroke weight everywhere (6 on a 64 grid, the stem weight of the wordmark), square corners and flat ends.
  • Two colours only: ink lines, paper fill. Reversed on ink for the app icon.
  • Basic shapes only: rectangles with tiny radii, circles only for lenses. No shading, no texture.
  • No ball: lenses carry the idea with a solid fill, a glint or a radar sweep (Gemini's point, and it holds).
