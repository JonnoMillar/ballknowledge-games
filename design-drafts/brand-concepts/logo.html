BK Scout logo: options

Five marks, each shown at 128, 64, 32 and 16 px, as an app icon, and next to the wordmark. Drawn in SVG, not generated, so they stay crisp at any size. Judge them at 32 and 16 first: a mark that survives there survives everywhere.

How complexity is kept down
  • One stroke weight everywhere (4.5 on a 64 grid, about 7% of the width), round joins.
  • Two colours only: ink lines, paper fill. Reversed on ink for the app icon.
  • Basic shapes only: circles, rounded rectangles, one curve. No shading, no texture.
  • The ball is one filled pentagon, the part of a ball everyone recognises; spokes are an option, and they are the first thing to go at small sizes.
