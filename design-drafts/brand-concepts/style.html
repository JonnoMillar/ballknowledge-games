Rules

  • FootballScouted everywhere: speech, watermarks, share cards. One word, capital F and S; never Football Scouted or FOOTBALLSCOUTED in running text.
  • Descriptor is fixed: Daily football games. Page titles carry it: FootballScouted · Daily football games.

  • Football in yellow on ink, ochre on paper; Scouted in cream on ink, ink on paper. Never other pairs.
  • No generated type, ever: the wordmark is real type plus the icon's fitted glasses.

2Colour

Colour system A: two primaries, ink for the big surfaces and cream for the second surface, and one rationed attention colour, yellow, used only on the main button, the Next chip and the Next flag. Red is retired. Meaning is always also carried by a word, shape or lightness; colour only ever repeats it.

4Line, grain and light

The art (rules for every generation)

  • Style: the grain illustration of concept 6 and the Olise test A: printed-grain texture, soft ink line, rich colour, no gloss.
  • Light: golden cool, from a window top left. Shadows fall down and right.
  • Palette: warm base, balanced toward ink and cream (dark blue-black accents, white paper, cool sky); no colour wash. Keep strong yellow out of the art so the layer's yellow stays the only call to act.
  • Never in the art: faces of real or made-up people, type, crests, logos, balls. Real badges come from ClubBadge on top.
  • Lock: one approved style-reference image goes into every generation, so pieces match.

The layer (so CSS pieces belong in the art)

  • Grain: paper surfaces carry the same tooth (SVG noise, multiply, 35%).
  • Shadow: two steps, contact plus soft, always down-right.
  • Tilt: pinned paper sits off-square, 0.5 to 2 degrees, never the same twice in a row.
  • Corners: paper 4 to 6 px, the clipboard 14 px; nothing pill-shaped.
