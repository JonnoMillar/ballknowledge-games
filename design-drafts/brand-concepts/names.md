## Round 4: wider styles (2026-11-15; Jonno rejected all of round 3)

Domains are .gg checks (all free unless noted). Clash-checked: Toepoke
(clean), Regista (cut: a football management sim and a football word game
already use it).

- **Fan slang:** Toepoke (the scrappy toe-end finish; playful, ownable),
  Worldies, Keepies, Fraud Watch (the "fraud" meme for overrated players).
- **Tactics words, the name itself a flex:** Half Space (the zone between
  the lines; also sounds like a place), Gegen, False Nine, Box to Box,
  Fantasista.
- **Match clock:** Added Time, Stoppage.
- **Short sounds:** Golo, Panna (street football's nutmeg).
- **Culture:** The 92 (every league club; UK-only).

## Round 3: coined words (Jonno: a unique word like Krillion, close to football)

Jonno: Scouted FC reads as a football club, which is wrong. "Daily" in the
name is out: generic, crowded ("Daily Football Quiz" exists), and it fences
out Rondo, the pub games and packs; it goes in the descriptor instead.

| Name | For | Against | Domain |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Footlore** | Football + lore: "lore" is 16 to 26 slang for knowing the backstory, so it means ball knowledge. Familiar shape (Footloose) helps it stick. Fits a room of files and clippings. Search finds nothing. | "Foot" alone can read as feet. | **footlore.gg free**; .com taken |
| **Ballion** | Ball + "-illion", the Krillion pattern; bullion hints at gold. "Done today's Ballion?" works. | Close to BallOn (a pitch-booking app); could be spelled Balion or Ballian. | **ballion.gg free**; .com taken |
| **Footwit** | Football + wit (and a nitwit pun for a wrong answer). | Reads a little like a joke name. | **footwit.com free** |

Cut in round 3: Knowtball (heard aloud it is just "football"), Goalden
(spelled Golden), Ballogy (a basketball app of that name), Kickle (kids'
game feel), Footsy (flirting), Dribbl (dropped vowel fails spell-once),
Pitchlore (Footlore says it better; "pitch" also means sales pitch). Also
found: a football quiz app already called Ball Knowledge.

## Cut, and why

- Wonderkid: a football career sim of that name launched Feb 2026.
- Tapped Up: bad readings (illegal approach; "tapped" = mad), weak daily sentence.
- Head Scout, Trialist: the obvious rank names.
- Shortlisted, Scout's Desk, Scoutfile, Scoutbook: sound like tools; Scoutbook is the Boy Scouts' software.
- Tipped: betting-tips reading.
- Dossier, Watchlist, Spotted, Touchline, Prospect, Radar: common words, domains taken, no football meaning of their own.
- Worldie, Top Bins: fan slang, but no link to the world.
- Ball Knowledge: mates called it weak; .gg taken.
- Recce, Hunch, Keen Eye, Ballwise: weak football or world fit.
