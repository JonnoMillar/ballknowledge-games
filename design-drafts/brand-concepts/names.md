# Hub name pool (2026-11-14)

Scored against `docs/naming-checklist.md`. Domain checks are registry
lookups on 2026-11-14 (nothing bought); a taken domain is not fatal if a
fixed modifier works (`___fc.com`). Clash checks are one web search each.

## Cut, and why

- Wonderkid: a football career sim of that name launched Feb 2026.
- Tapped Up: bad readings (illegal approach; "tapped" = mad), weak daily sentence.
- Head Scout, Trialist: the obvious rank names.
- Shortlisted, Scout's Desk, Scoutfile, Scoutbook: sound like tools; Scoutbook is the Boy Scouts' software.
- Tipped: betting-tips reading.
- Dossier, Watchlist, Spotted, Touchline, Prospect, Radar: common words, domains taken, no football meaning of their own.
- Worldie, Top Bins: fan slang, but no link to the world.
- Ball Knowledge: mates called it weak; .gg taken.
- Recce, Hunch, Keen Eye, Ballwise: weak football or world fit.
